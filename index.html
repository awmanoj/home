I'm an engineering leader and programmer with two decades of building software, from systems code at Adobe to the engineering organisations behind some of Southeast Asia's largest consumer platforms. Today I lead technology at Cleartrip, one of India's largest travel companies.

Before this I was CTO at JULO, a fintech lending to Indonesia's underbanked, and spent six years at Tokopedia growing from Technical Architect to SVP of Engineering. I am deeply passionate about technology, read widely, and still write code most days, mostly small tools that do one thing well.
